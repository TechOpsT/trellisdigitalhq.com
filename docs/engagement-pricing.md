# Trellis Digital — Internal Engagement Pricing Guardrails

> Internal planning document. Do not publish to the marketing site without an explicit decision.

## Purpose

Use these ranges to keep early engagements commercially disciplined while Trellis Digital builds its client base. Final pricing should reflect scope, delivery risk, timeline, third-party costs, and support obligations.

| Engagement | Internal target |
| --- | ---: |
| Discovery / advisory | $250–$750 |
| Small implementation | $750–$2,500 |
| Integration / automation | $1,500–$5,000 |
| Custom application | $3,500+ |
| Ongoing consulting | Hourly or monthly retainer |

## Commercial rules

- Use a written scope before implementation begins.
- Separate discovery from implementation when requirements are uncertain.
- Require explicit approval for third-party software, cloud, messaging, domain, or merchant costs.
- Use milestone payments for larger projects; 50/50 is acceptable for small fixed-scope work when cash-flow risk is low.
- Do not absorb recurring client infrastructure costs into a one-time implementation fee unless intentionally priced as managed service.
- Define support and change-request boundaries in the proposal.
- Do not discount below a sustainable delivery rate solely to win work; document any strategic first-client discount separately from standard value.
